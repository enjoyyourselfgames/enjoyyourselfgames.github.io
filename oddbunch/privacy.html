Privacy Policy

Odd Bunch: Picture Connect (Android) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Odd Bunch: Picture Connect ("the game"), made by Enjoy Yourself Games ("we"), uses, and why. If you have a question, write to enjoyyourselfabout@gmail.com.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not run servers that collect information about you. Your progress (boards, stars, coins, hints, your Daily Board streak, your settings and your ads and stats choices) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) and Unity Ads. To show, count and limit ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and how you interact with ads.

Some ads are optional: you choose to watch them for a reward (a free hint, extra coins or a streak save). Other ads may appear between boards.

The first time you play, the game asks whether ads may be picked for you. If you say yes, our partners may show personalised ads; if you say no, they show general (non-personalised) ads. You can change your answer at any time in the game's settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most Android phones).

Our ad partners handle this information under their own policies: Unity / LevelPlay privacy policy.

Play stats and crash reports

The first time you play, the game also asks whether it may send anonymous play stats and crash reports. Only if you say yes, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (made by Google) to send us information such as which boards are started, won or left, how long a board takes, which hints and shop items are used, which optional ads are watched, and crash reports (what went wrong, the device model and Android version). This comes with an app instance ID made by Firebase, not your name or email; the advertising ID is not collected for stats. We use it only to find bugs and to make the boards better, never to show ads.

If you say no, nothing is sent. You can change your answer at any time in the game's settings (Stats choice). The game also downloads a few settings from Firebase Remote Config (such as how often ads appear), so we can adjust them without an update.

Google handles this information under its own policies: Firebase privacy and security.

Purchases

Hints and other items in the game's shop are bought with coins earned in the game. If the game ever offers purchases with real money, they are handled by Google Play: we never see your payment details, and Google tells the game only what you bought, so it can be given to you.

Permissions

The game uses the internet to load ads, its settings and (only if you agree) to send stats, and your phone's vibration (you can turn vibration off in the game's settings). It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child's information reached us, write to us and we will help remove it.

Google Play

Google Play may give us general statistics about the game (such as installs and crashes) that do not identify you. See Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
