Privacy Policy

WHOSE? Lost & Found (Android) · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game WHOSE? Lost & Found ("the game"), made by Enjoy Yourself Games ("we"), uses, and why. If you have a question, write to enjoyyourselfabout@gmail.com.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not run servers that collect information about you. Your progress (levels, stamps, tips, your ads choice) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) and Unity Ads. To show, count and limit ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and how you interact with ads.

The first time you play, the game asks whether ads may be picked for you. If you say yes, our partners may show personalised ads; if you say no, they show general (non-personalised) ads. You can change your answer at any time with the TV button in the game's settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most Android phones).

Our ad partners handle this information under their own policies: Unity / LevelPlay privacy policy.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google tells the game only what you bought, so it can be given to you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Google Play

Google Play may give us general statistics about the game (such as installs and crashes) that do not identify you. See Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
